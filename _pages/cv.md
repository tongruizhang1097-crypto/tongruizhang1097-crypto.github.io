## Research Interests

<p>My current doctoral interests center on spatial intelligence for embodied systems: visual perception, localization, spatial representation, sensor fusion, map matching, and mobile-robot navigation. I am especially interested in methods that remain reliable when observations are incomplete or environments change.</p>
